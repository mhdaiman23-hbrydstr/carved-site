Delete your Carved account

In the app: open Carved, go to More, then Delete account, and confirm. Your account and everything synced to it (profile, plan, meal choices, recipes, your foods, weigh-ins, adjustments and cardio log) are removed from our servers straight away.

Without the app: email mhd.aiman23@gmail.com from the address you signed in with and ask us to delete your account. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Nothing is kept after deletion apart from backups held by our database host, which roll off within their standard retention period. Your one-time purchase stays with your Apple or Google account and can be restored if you sign up again.
