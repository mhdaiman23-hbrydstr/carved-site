Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 October 2026

Carved is a nutrition planning app run by SocialSquare ("we"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: we collect what the app needs to build and save your plan, we do not sell it, and we do not use it for ads.

What we collect

DataWhy
Email address, and your name if Apple or Google shares it, through Sign in with Apple or GoogleTo create your account and keep your data with it
Body and fitness numbers: sex, height, weight, body fat, waist, training days and timesTo calculate your targets and track your trend
Plan data: goal, meal choices, recipes, foods you add, dietary preferences, cardio sessions, plan adjustmentsTo show and sync your plan
Purchase status: whether you unlocked Carved, through Apple or Google and our purchase providerTo unlock the app and restore it on your devices

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or browsing history. The app has no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Where it is stored

Your data is kept on your device and synced to our database hosted by Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). Purchases are handled by Apple or Google and verified through RevenueCat, which receives your account identifier and purchase history but not your health numbers. Sign-in is provided by Apple or Google under their own privacy policies.

How we use it

Only to run the app: calculating your plan, syncing it between your devices, restoring your purchase and answering you when you contact us. We do not sell your data, share it for advertising, or use it to train models.

Health data

Weight, body fat and waist are health and fitness data. They are used only to calculate and adjust your plan. Carved is not a medical device and does not give medical advice.

Your choices

  • Export: Settings, Export data gives you everything as a file.
  • Delete: Settings, Delete account removes your account and all synced data from our servers straight away. Your purchase stays with your Apple or Google account.
  • Correct: edit your numbers in the app at any time.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you have rights to access, correct, delete and port your data, and to complain to a data protection authority. Email us to use any of them.

Retention

We keep your data while you have an account. When you delete your account it is removed from our database; backups held by our host roll off within their standard retention period.

Children

Carved is for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
